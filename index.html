About Us

Empowering Your Global Education Journey

REIGN STUDY HUB LTD is an international education consultancy dedicated to helping students access quality educational opportunities in reputable institutions around the world. As a subsidiary of PIS INT'L SYNERGY LTD, we guide prospective students through the study-abroad process — from choosing suitable universities and programmes to preparing admission applications and understanding student visa requirements.

We understand that pursuing an education abroad is a significant investment in your future. Our goal is to make the process more organised, accessible and understandable by providing guidance tailored to your academic background, career interests, destination preferences and financial circumstances.
